> ADRs are **immutable history.** Once Accepted, an ADR is not edited — if the decision changes,
> write a new ADR that supersedes it and flip this one's status to "Superseded by ADR-XXXX." This
> is the third pillar of the Canon (see the Engineering Constitution).

## Context
The forces at play: the problem, constraints, requirements, and what made a decision necessary.
State things as they were *at decision time*.

## Decision
The choice made, in active voice ("We will…"). Be specific enough that a new contributor
understands exactly what was adopted.

## Alternatives considered
- **Option A** — why it was/wasn't chosen.
- **Option B** — …

## Consequences
- **Positive:** what this buys us.
- **Negative / trade-offs:** what it costs us (be honest — this is the most valuable section in a year).
- **Neutral:** follow-ons, things to watch.

## References
Files, commits, docs, or external links that ground this decision.
